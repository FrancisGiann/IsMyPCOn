# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-directed: static-hostable React, Vite, and TypeScript, matching the existing web-project toolchain. No server or paid service.

## Users

People who momentarily doubt whether their desktop computer is running and would enjoy an absurdly solemn answer. A phone visitor is also part of the joke.

## Product Purpose

Deliver one dramatically overproduced piece of reassurance: “yes, your PC is on.” Success means the joke is clear in seconds and the check feels satisfying to use.

## Positioning

A knowingly unnecessary status company that performs theatrical diagnostics without accessing the computer. The answer is a gag, not a measurement.

## Operating Context

A single-page marketing and interaction experience for static hosting, designed for desktop and phone browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Run a short three-stage theatrical check and show a prominent result. The result is always a joke; no hardware is inspected.
- Recognize phone-sized/touch visits on a best-effort basis and include “Sir, this is a phone.”
- Show the monthly free-check notice but never block replay. If saved locally, the usage marker stays in this browser and is not sent anywhere.
- Pro and Enterprise interactions are clearly fictional. A fake checkout always shows a total of ₱0. No payment, account, email, or sales request is collected.
- No backend, device permissions, external APIs, autoplay sound, or real billing.

## Brand Commitments

Use the exact name “Is My PC On?”; keep the company tone absurdly serious, dry, premium, and funny.

## Evidence on Hand

No real diagnostic capability, product metrics, customers, endorsements, contact details, or sales operation. Do not imply any of these exist. Prices are explicitly parody.

## Product Principles

1. Make the answer immediate and legible.
2. Make the drama much larger than the diagnostic.
3. Be honest that nothing has inspected the computer.
4. Keep every interaction local, replayable, and harmless.
