# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-directed: static-hostable React, Vite, and TypeScript, matching the existing web-project toolchain. No server or paid service.

## Users

People who want to check whether their desktop computer is running.

## Product Purpose

Present a clear PC status-check experience with visible subscription options and accurate capability disclosures.

## Positioning

A PC status-check interface. The current static site cannot access a computer or determine its power state, so it keeps the result locked.

## Operating Context

A single-page marketing and interaction experience for static hosting, designed for desktop and phone browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Run a short three-stage status sequence. The page does not inspect hardware, and the result stays locked.
- Estimate phone-sized/touch visits from browser and screen details for a mobile disclosure.
- List Pro at ₱99/month and Enterprise at custom pricing.
- State clearly that online checkout and sales contact are not available yet. Do not claim that a payment, subscription, or sales inquiry succeeded.
- No backend, device permissions, external APIs, autoplay sound, or payment processing.

## Brand Commitments

Use the exact name “Is My PC On?” and keep the visual style clear, calm, and premium.

## Evidence on Hand

The current site has no diagnostic capability, product metrics, customers, endorsements, contact details, payment processor, or sales operation. Do not imply these exist. Pro costs ₱99/month; Enterprise pricing is custom.

## Product Principles

1. Make the status and plan prices easy to understand.
2. Disclose that no computer information is accessed.
3. Keep payment and sales availability accurate.
4. Keep every interaction local and harmless.
